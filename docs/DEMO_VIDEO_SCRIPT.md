# 60-second demo video script

Record at 1440x900, browser zoom 100%, light mode, signed in as Dr. Maya Ellison. Start the auto-simulator
(every 8 seconds) a minute before recording so the feed is moving. Keep the Live data demo page open in a second tab
with a QR code showing.

| Time | Screen | Action | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:06 | Sign-in page | Click Dr. Maya Ellison, sign in | "Reflect clients do art therapy in VR and on their phones. Their provider needs to see what happens between sessions, and get paid for reviewing it." |
| 0:06-0:16 | Dashboard | Point at the stat strip, then "Supports in billing" | "This is Dr. Ellison's caseload: who's on remote therapeutic monitoring, which codes are ready, and about how much that supports in billing this month." |
| 0:16-0:24 | Dashboard > Needs attention | A safety toast arrives (send PHQ-9 item 9 from the other tab just before) | "Safety comes first. A client just endorsed item 9 on the PHQ-9, so every open page alerts the provider within seconds." |
| 0:24-0:34 | Client page, Artwork then Therapeutic response | Click the client, open Artwork, then the mood chart | "Here's her work in Reflect, the emotions she chose, and how her mood changes before and after each session." |
| 0:34-0:42 | Client page, press Ctrl + Shift | The wave opens the assistant; ask "What should I bring up today?" | "The assistant answers from this client's data only. Names and identifiers never leave our server." |
| 0:42-0:50 | Client page, RTM & billing | Show the review-time tracker and meters, click Attest | "While she reviews, the dashboard tracks her active time. She attests it, and the rules engine shows exactly which 2026 CPT codes it supports." |
| 0:50-0:56 | Live data demo, QR card | Count goes up as a phone check-in arrives | "And it's live: scan the code, and your check-in shows up on her screen." |
| 0:56-1:00 | Dashboard, Outcomes tab | Hold on the before/after chart | "Reflect Provider Dashboard: better care between sessions, and a way to pay for it." |

Tips
- Do the phone check-in for real on camera (screen-record the phone too and put it picture-in-picture).
- Pre-pick a client with good data (Imani Brooks: response on PHQ-9, many art pieces).
- If the AI key isn't set, the assistant still answers in offline mode; say "connected to Groq" only if it is.
- Export at 1080p, captions on (many judges watch muted).
